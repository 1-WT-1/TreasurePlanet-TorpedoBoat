[center][b]Treasure Planet Torpedo Boat[/b][/center]

A recreation of a vessel from the 21st-century video game, "Treasure Planet: Battle at Procyon". This "Age of Sail" design utilizes a minimal thruster layout; its agility comes from the exceptionally wide gimbal range on both its main engine and its two maneuvering thrusters.
